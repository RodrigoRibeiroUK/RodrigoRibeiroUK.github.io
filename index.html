About Me

Strategy, technology and creativity working together.

My professional journey has always been multidisciplinary. Over more than two decades, I have worked across business administration, finance, operations, team management, entrepreneurship and technology — experiences that shaped the way I approach projects today.

I hold a Bachelor's Degree in Business Administration and an MBA in Investments & Asset Allocation, combining a strong business and financial foundation with an ongoing interest in technology, innovation and problem-solving.

Over the years, I have founded and developed businesses and digital platforms, managed teams and operations, worked with financial markets, and increasingly focused my career on technology-led projects.

Today, my work spans digital product development, artificial intelligence, automation, algorithmic trading systems, data-driven workflows, web experiences, branding and visual design.

Rather than seeing strategy, technology and design as separate disciplines, I use them together. I enjoy taking an idea from its earliest stage, understanding the problem behind it, structuring the business logic, designing the experience and finding practical ways to turn it into something real.

I'm naturally curious and passionate about what I do. I continuously learn, experiment and explore new technologies — particularly AI and automation — not simply because they are new, but because of what they can enable when combined with business knowledge, creativity and real-world experience.
